== Objective

Explain who decides and what each piece of evidence can establish.

== Learner actions

. Explain the four outcomes in one minute, always prefacing fixture results with “In this rehearsal example”.
. Name the policy owner who approves rules and permitted context; the platform operator who enforces them, proves placement and recovers failures; and the business owner who approves a bounded next workload.
. Explain that Granite produces text after authorization. It cannot change policy, choose destinations, authorize infrastructure or accept its own answer.
. Record one next workload, the evidence it would require and its human approver role. Do not record a real sensitive prompt.

The ledger stores and checks records; it grants no governance authority. A green chain result does not approve production use. Pattern scores are configured values, not measured model quality.
Decision, execution and evidence remain separate: allow can precede failed inference, and an enforced deny can lack acknowledged evidence.

== Learner checkpoint

*Pass when:* your worksheet contains the ownership explanation and a conditional next-workload proposal with an approver. This is a rehearsal learning checkpoint, not deployment approval or certification.
