== Objective

Turn the technical evidence into a bounded approval decision.

== Why it matters

Sovereign AI is an operating decision, not a product label. The organization still needs people who own policy, platform enforcement, recovery, and workload approval.

== Do it

Review the evidence summary and record the accountable human role.

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
printf '\n=== Evidence summary ===\n'
for result in general sensitive injection outage; do
  jq -r '"\(.request_id): class=\(.classification) decision=\(.decision) execution=\(.execution_status) dispatches=\(.model_dispatch_count) evidence=\(.evidence_status)"' \
    "$HOME/sovereign-ai-101/$result.json"
done

sed -i 's|- Human owner:.*|- Human owner: policy owner approves rules; platform operator enforces and recovers; business owner approves use|' "$HOME/sovereign-ai-101/decision-brief.md"
sed -i 's|- Reviewer decision:.*|- Reviewer decision: APPROVE THIS REHEARSAL PATTERN; PRODUCTION REMAINS UNPROVEN|' "$HOME/sovereign-ai-101/decision-brief.md"
cat "$HOME/sovereign-ai-101/decision-brief.md"
----

== What happened

You approved a behavior pattern, not a production deployment. The run showed deterministic policy handling and verifiable rehearsal receipts. It did not prove live model identity, Intel CPU placement, geographic residency, production security, or compliance.

== Learner checkpoint

*Pass when:* you can explain this conclusion in one sentence: “Policy decides, generation executes only after permission, evidence records the result, and a human owns approval.”
