== Objective

Prevent old or another learner’s receipts from satisfying this session.

== Learner actions

. Inspect `baseline`: the simulated session starts at `2000-01-01T00:00:00Z`, and the baseline is captured at scenario minute 3. These are teaching-clock times, not observations made today.
. Copy the five type-chain tips, their entry IDs, hashes and positions into the worksheet baseline section. All tips are at position 1.
. Identify the six expected new receipts: two general-allow phases, two sensitive-allow phases, one denial and one abstention.
. Clear any prior worksheet result before proceeding. Reusing a presenter’s receipts is not learner evidence.

*Blocked live procedure:* before any request, record actual session start, server baseline time, known entry IDs and per-type tips through the authenticated read-only viewer. Use newly allocated session-bound IDs, not prompt markers.
The internal gateway supports filtered reads, but the reviewed public proxy does not establish session-authorized forwarding of filters.

== Learner checkpoint

*Pass when:* baseline IDs are distinct from all six later receipt IDs, and you can explain why a timestamp or a larger entry count alone cannot prove freshness. This rehearses G06.
